FuseGroup ​

The Fuse Group is a safety and control grouping. It describes which Zendure devices are connected to the same fuse or circuit and what maximum power is permitted there.

responsibility

The correct selection of the Fuse Group is relevant to safety. Choose them to suit your electrical installation and local regulations.

Why it is important ​

Zendure Manager distributes power based on the fuse group setting. Devices in the same group together should not exceed the set limit.

Without a suitable fuse group, a device will not be used for smart functions.

Typical options ​

optionMeaning
Nicht verwendetDevice has no fuse group and is not used for smart matching
Eigener Stromkreis / eigene PhaseDevice is alone on a dedicated circuit
